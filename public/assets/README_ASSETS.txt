# Cute Fantasy RPG — Organized Assets

This archive combines the supplied Cute Fantasy graphics pack and audio pack.

## Contents
- Graphics: 24 files (player, enemies, animals, tiles, outdoor decorations, and original read_me.txt)
- Music: 1 file(s)
- Sound effects: 62 file(s)
- Attribution/reference files: 1 file(s)

## Folder layout
- Graphics/ — original graphics organized by their original folders
- Audio/Music/ — background/battle music
- Audio/Sound_Effects/ — sound effects
- Audio/Attribution/ — attribution/reference URL files

## File integrity
- Audio files were not converted, edited, normalized, resampled, or re-encoded.
- Graphics files were not edited or resized.
- The ZIP container uses ZIP_STORED (no additional ZIP compression).
- One duplicate copy of `Fair_Fight_(Battle).wav` was found in two locations in the source audio archive. Only one copy is included to avoid redundant duplication; the audio bytes are unchanged.

## Important
Check and follow the original licenses/attribution requirements for both asset packs before publishing or distributing the game. The graphics pack's included read_me.txt is preserved.
